### Slide 3: Problem Statement — The 4 Critical Operational Bottlenecks
* **Slide on Screen:** 4 Red Pain Point Cards (Highway Blind Spots, 45-min Dispatch, Dead-Zones, 14-Day Billing Lag).
* **Target Time:** 2:15 – 3:45 (90s)
* **Visual Cue:** Walk across the 4 pain points, connecting operational frustrations to business loss.

#### 🗣️ What to Say:
> *"Before TrackPoint, NorthLine was constrained by four critical industry bottlenecks:*
>
> * **First, Stuart Highway Blind Spots:** Once a triple road train pulled out of the Darwin depot, dispatchers and customers were effectively blind for 15 hours. Shippers constantly called dispatch desks asking 'Where is my freight?', disrupting operations.*
> * **Second, 45-Minute Dispatch Latency:** Matching freight manifests to trucks was done manually on physical whiteboards and spreadsheets. This led to underutilized trailers, missed departures, and delayed emergency shipments.*
> * **Third, Remote Cellular Dead-Zones:** Standard cloud apps crashed the moment drivers drove into outstation blackspots. Drivers had to rely on paper dockets that got dirty, torn, or lost in the cab.*
> * **Fourth, The 14-Day Invoicing Lag:** Finance could not invoice commercial clients until the physical paper Proof-of-Delivery docket returned to Darwin two weeks later. This tied up hundreds of thousands of dollars in working capital and inflated Days Sales Outstanding.*
>
> *These weren't just minor inconveniences — they directly impaired NorthLine’s profitability."*

## 🎯 Anticipated Q&A & Executive Defense

| Question from Audience | Best Spoken Response |
|---|---|
| **Q1: "How does the app work when a driver is completely offline between Katherine and Tennant Creek?"** | *"TrackPoint utilizes an offline-first HTML5 canvas buffer. When the device detects no network, it saves the signature PNG and metadata locally in browser IndexedDB/LocalStorage. As soon as the vehicle reaches 4G coverage or enters a depot Wi-Fi zone, the handset automatically background-syncs the payload to `/api/jobs/[id]`."* |
| **Q2: "Why use 15-second polling instead of WebSockets for live vehicle tracking?"** | *"In remote outstation linehauls, continuous WebSocket connections suffer frequent TCP socket drops, reconnection storms, and battery drain on mobile handsets. Serverless 15-second REST polling guarantees 100% reliability, sub-second latency, and zero connection dropouts on serverless cloud infrastructure (NFR-02)."* |
| **Q3: "How does the system ensure dispatchers don't get bypassed by automated algorithms?"** | *"While TrackPoint provides automated nearest-vehicle recommendations based on GPS coordinates and trailer capacity, the dispatcher retains full operational authority through the Admin Console modal (`AdminConsignmentModal.tsx`) to manually reassign prime movers, adjust priority tiers, or update manifest notes at any time."* |
| **Q4: "How does this benefit B2B customer accounting departments?"** | *"Traditional carriers create a 14-day delay waiting for paper delivery slips. TrackPoint generates an official Australian Tax Invoice PDF with itemized 10% GST and embedded receiver signature the exact second delivery is signed, allowing immediate 3-way matching and accounts payable release."* |
